01 What we found, what we changed

Nine things costing you calls right now.

  1. Today

    All 21 service links lead to an error

    Every service on your home page (Brake Repair, Oil Changes, A/C Repair and the rest) links to texastirescustoms.com. That address isn't connected to a website right now, so customers who click get a "site can't be reached" error. Your email on that domain still works.

    New site

    Every service is on the same site, grouped and described, so nobody lands on a dead page.

  2. Today

    No call button on the first phone screen

    Most people looking for tires are on their phone and want to call. Right now they have to scroll to find the number.

    New site

    A call button in the header, the hero and the footer, plus a Call / Directions / Quote bar pinned to the bottom on phones.

  3. Today

    The Auto Repair page shows a blank box

    texastires9.com/autorepair holds one embedded quote form from another company. When we opened it, the form didn't load and the page showed an empty gray box.

    New site

    All 27 services, grouped into Tires, Wheels and lifts, Auto repair and Maintenance, each with a plain one-line description.

  4. Today

    Small details customers notice

    "Engine Diagnositcs" is misspelled in the services list, and Privacy Policy sits in the main menu between Contact and Financing.

    New site

    A short menu built around what customers want: services, tire finder, financing, our work, locations and a quote. Legal links live in the footer.

  5. Today

    Two different financing offers

    The home page says "100 day same as cash." The About section says "90 days same as cash."

    New site

    One clear financing section with your four providers and a three-step "how it works." We left the day count out until you confirm the right number.

  6. Today

    No way to look up a tire size

    Customers have to call just to ask if you carry their size.

    New site

    A tire size finder: type the size from the sidewall and it explains the numbers, then sends that size with the quote request.

  7. Today

    English only

    Many drivers in Haltom City and Fort Worth prefer Spanish.

    New site

    One tap switches the whole page to Spanish, including the tire finder and the quote form.

  8. Today

    Hours are hard to find

    Opening hours sit near the bottom of the page, inside the contact form area.

    New site

    A live "Open now" or "Closed" badge on Texas time at the top of the page, plus both shops with a large map and one-tap directions.

  9. Today

    A heavy page to load

    The current home page loads about 2.4 MB across 191 files on a computer.

    New site

    Hand-built and lightweight, so it opens fast even on a weak phone signal. See the numbers below.
